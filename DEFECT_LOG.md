# Defect Tracking Log - Pharmacy POS System
**Group 16 - Advanced Software Engineering (CSC4630)**

Severity is judged by patient-safety and data-confidentiality impact first, then
by disruption to the cashier workflow. A defect is only marked RESOLVED once an
automated test in `server/src/__tests__/` fails without the fix.

Where that is genuinely not possible, the entry says so in its own words rather
than implying a test that does not exist. **DEF-053 is the only such entry**: it
concerns what happens on first boot against an empty production database, and
reproducing it needs a freshly provisioned database per run, which the suite
does not have. It is closed on inspection and a production-mode boot, and the
entry states that plainly. Every other RESOLVED entry is break-tested.

| **DEF-064** | 2026-09-06 | POS / Receipt (UI) | The receipt printed figures the browser had made up rather than the ones the server recorded and charged. `handleCompleteSale` in `POSCheckout.jsx` built `soldTotals` from the cart store's own arithmetic — `getVat()` and `getGrandTotal()` apply a flat **16% to the whole basket** — and passed that to the receipt, discarding the `subtotal`, `tax_amount` and `total` the sale response carries. ### Limitations since closed

Four limitations previously carried here have been fixed rather than left
stated. They are recorded so the history is legible.

| ID | Was | Closed by |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LIM-001** | Checkout confirmed a prescription was supplied, not that it listed the drug being sold. | The guard now matches each prescription-only line against `prescription_items` and refuses a quantity above the amount prescribed. `catalogueAndGuards.test.js` covers both refusals. |
| **LIM-002** | `dispenseStock` recorded a movement even when the scoped batch update matched nothing, logging a phantom dispense. | The batch is now selected `FOR UPDATE` and checked for ownership, expiry and sufficient stock before anything is written; a mismatch rolls back with nothing recorded. `adjustStock` gained the same treatment and now requires a reason, since an unexplained adjustment is indistinguishable from shrinkage. |
| **LIM-004** | A sale belonged to a cashier but not to a shift. There was no float, no closing count and no cash variance, so a drawer could be short and nothing in the system would say so — the stock-and-cash loss the Inception business case set out to reduce, and the one control the system did not have. | `till_sessions` records the float in, the takings recorded, the cash counted and the difference between the last two, and `sales.till_session_id` binds each sale to the shift that took it. Two rules make the reconciliation mean something, and both are enforced by the server: expected cash is computed from the payments actually recorded and is never accepted from the request body, and only `cash` payments count towards the drawer, since a card or insurance settlement never puts a note in the till. A partial unique index prevents a cashier holding two open drawers, and the close is taken under a row lock so two people cannot write two variances over each other. `require_till_session` makes the control mandatory per pharmacy; with it on, a sale outside an open shift is refused and rolls back whole. Covered by `tillSession.test.js` — 15 cases, 8 of which fail if either rule is removed. |
| **LIM-003** | Six controllers answered with invented data when PostgreSQL was unreachable — a queue of fabricated patients, a prescription that was never written, a tenant list naming a pharmacy that might not exist. | Every fallback is removed. A controller that cannot reach the database returns `503 DATABASE_UNAVAILABLE`. An outage now looks like an outage, which is what the health check exists to establish. |
